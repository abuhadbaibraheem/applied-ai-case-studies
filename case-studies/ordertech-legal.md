# OrderTech Legal — a connected workspace for law offices

**Public product:** [OrderTech for law offices](https://lawyers.ordertech.ai/) · Arabic/English · Web and mobile

## The problem

A legal team works across client records, cases, documents, appointments, tasks, and billing. When these live in separate tools, people lose context and spend time collecting it again. AI can help with intake and drafting, but the team still needs control over access, review, and the final decision.

## Product approach

OrderTech brings the office's day-to-day work into a connected workspace. The public product includes case and client management, document context, calendars, billing, team roles, and an office website. It also describes an assistant for initial client enquiries and an in-workspace assistant for summaries and draft preparation.

## Engineering principles I focus on

1. **Keep related records connected.** A client, case, document, appointment, and payment should be understandable in context.
2. **Respect each person's access.** An assistant should use the same permissions as the person requesting help.
3. **Keep a human in the loop.** Drafts need review; consequential actions need a clear proposal and confirmation.
4. **Make hand-off visible.** The office team must be able to take over a client conversation when needed.
5. **Keep offices separate.** The product direction gives each office its own identity and workspace.

## What I would verify

Role-based access, cross-office separation, Arabic/English flows, mobile and web consistency, source grounding for AI answers, and clear confirmation and recovery paths. This note reflects the public product description and engineering approach; it does not publish private source code or claim that every integration is active in every office.
